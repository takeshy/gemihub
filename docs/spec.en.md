# gemihub specification

[Japanese source](spec.md) · [English README](../README.md)

This is the English counterpart of the staged requirements and later refinements in `spec.md`. Later refinements take precedence over early roadmap ideas. The initial roadmap names Go, React, Google Cloud/Firestore/Drive, Storybook, and Terraform; the current repository implements its backend in TypeScript/Node.js. The final voice requirements use browser dictation, not the earlier bidirectional voice concept.

## Product and promises

Create a dedicated booking system for a small salon, beauty practice, bodywork business, private lesson studio, or similar independent shop through a conversation with its nontechnical owner. Begin with Google login, use plain language, and keep business records, credentials and operational state in Firestore, with images and page files in the owner's Drive. Owners can export records as JSON or CSV. The owner retains readable specifications and history even if the service ends.

When the owner enables it in booking rules, customers pay the full amount through PayPal at booking, and cancellations are refunded automatically under the policy saved at booking. Record no-shows without charging anything further; shops that have not enabled payment keep operating without it. Record cancellations and no-shows without deleting their rows. Customers see only their own bookings. Only the owner and registered staff who completed Google login/calendar consent can operate a shop. Require on-screen review and explicit application of page/design changes; never treat speech as approval. Dedicated management saves apply live business data. Record changes in history.

Every screen must be reviewable in Storybook, including staff preference/no preference and disconnected, connected, own-calendar, and removed-staff states. Group stories by management tasks, customer registration/booking/My page/additional pages, articles, Calendar, and help. Include an illustrated manual with fictional sample data.

## Same-day phone bookings

Operators add a booking for today when a customer telephones, paid in cash at the shop. A name and phone number are required. Customers cannot create one. With prepayment on, only today's slots in the shop's time zone are accepted, so cash bookings cannot hold later slots. With prepayment off, online bookings are unpaid as well, so operators can choose any date within the booking window. They use the same availability as online bookings, including pending payments, connected calendars and slots awaiting a replacement customer, so this form does not offer those slots. A registered number fills in the name without replacing a typed one. An operator can give a slot awaiting a replacement to a cash customer if it is today, has not started and no one is paying online: the original booking is cancelled with the refund promised for a replacement instead of the lower cancellation amount, and a phone booking for the same slot is created at the listing price in one transaction. Cash collected equals the raised refund, so the shop's total is unchanged when the customer pays; if they do not arrive, the shop bears that amount. Online same-day and minimum-notice rules do not apply. The booking registers the customer in Customers under that number, and Customers can also register someone by name and phone alone. Numbers are normalized across width and punctuation, so visits, totals and notes accumulate on one customer. With no account, they receive no email and are outside coupons, refunds and replacement listings, and are not merged automatically with an online member. Owners can cancel or record a no-show as usual.

## Customer bookings

Coupon management opens on the active/paused list, with separate creation and manual-distribution work areas. Creation offers purpose-based templates and rejects incompatible conditions in both UI and API. Successful creation closes the form and focuses the completion notice; a retry retains its operation ID. Manual recipients remain visible and removable independently of search filters, next to a submit button showing the count. My page prioritizes available coupons, offers Book with this coupon to carry the selection into booking, includes expiry in the booking selector, and collapses used/expired history.

Owners manage coupons in a dedicated Coupons screen. Eligibility combines all/any of first visit (zero ended active bookings), birthday month, an exact visit count / every N visits / at least N visits, and all members. Choose 1–100% or a fixed shop-currency amount and 1–365 days of validity. Calculate on registration (existing members once too), after visits ending since campaign creation (counted 24 hours after the booking ends so no-shows can be recorded first, and issued once per visit number), monthly at a local day/hour, or once on a specified anniversary date. Run every minute with durable period/visit deduplication; clamp nonexistent month days to month end and DST gaps to the next valid local minute. Members registering after a monthly or one-time distribution time are excluded, and a monthly campaign starts from its first scheduled time after creation. AI turns requests into editable proposals and asks for missing anniversary dates; only the owner's explicit enable action creates a campaign. Pausing stops new distribution while preserving issued coupons; resuming does not catch up on visits or dates inside the pause.

Manual grants let the owner search registered shop members, select one or more, and set a title, percentage/fixed discount and validity. Keep the operation ID, author and frozen grant details; resume partial batches without double grants on retries. Manual grants share the customer wallet and redemption flow with automatic grants.

Show coupons on My page and let customers choose one in the booking summary. Calculate discounts on services plus add-ons in integer yen/cents, capped at the total. Validate the discounted consent and charge that amount through PayPal; fully discounted bookings need no payment. Reserve coupons during pending payment, retain ambiguous captures, release expired/abandoned unpaid holds, and consume on confirmation. Completed normal cancellations, including shop cancellations, restore an unexpired coupon without extending its original expiry. No-shows and customer cancellations at or after the start time do not restore coupons. Pending replacement listings keep the coupon consumed until the replacement sale or final cancellation completes; it returns to the original customer, not the buyer. Usage and returns are journaled per booking so retries cannot consume a returned coupon or release a later use, and confirmation rejects a coupon another booking is using. Refunds never exceed cash paid. Do not stack with discounted cancellation slots. Freeze grant terms and booking discount snapshots, preserve usage after holder changes, and export campaign/grant/use/manual records in shop JSON. Practice and offline flows never change real coupon records.

Send customers a pre-visit email with appointment times in the shop time zone, services/add-ons, staff, address and a normal authenticated My page link for review/cancellation. Normally send 24 hours before; for unpaid bookings use one hour before the existing cancellation cutoff if earlier. Explain the timing on the receipt and encourage early cancellation. Exclude bookings created or bought by transfer after their reminder time, bookings awaiting a replacement, unconfirmed payments, cancelled/no-show/started bookings and archived shops. Run every minute and catch up after downtime only before the appointment starts. Commit a durable deduplication marker and outbox job together under the mail lock, retry failures, and recheck the current holder/start/status before delivery. Save the booking language (Japanese/English), falling back to the shop language for old records. Judge shops record messages without delivery; practice and offline screens do not send mail.

Signed-out visitors, owner accounts, and customers of another shop see a sign-in prompt instead of booking inputs, slots, or payment actions. Show the form and fetch slots only after signing in as this shop’s customer and completing member registration.

After receiving the confirmed booking record, replace the input form with a dedicated completion screen showing the saved date/time, customer, actual assigned person, services and add-ons, total, duration, booking number and message. Offer My page and another booking. Use the same screen after confirmed PayPal payment; never show completion for pending/failed payment, missing booking data or another customer's booking. Rechecks and payment-return reloads must not create duplicate charges or bookings. Previews and offline Drive storefronts use the same completion screen.

Show About the shop before login and a unified Register/Login panel below the header for new/existing customers. Explicit booking links still open booking. After login, My page is the first and leftmost destination with recent shop news and personal bookings.

Customers choose a service, a qualified staff member or No preference, then date/time. Default to no preference; there is no owner-side booking-method toggle. Named selection checks only that person and never falls back to another. No preference assigns an actually available qualified person at confirmation. Changing service/staff/date clears the slot and refreshes availability; keep no preference across service changes and reset an incompatible staff choice.

Validate opening hours, staff shifts, service duration, closures, existing active bookings, lead time, same-day policy, advance limit, and connected Calendar busy times. Recheck immediately before confirmation under the shop lock. No booking on a closed day or overlapping an occupied slot. Show assigned person, recorded price, status, booking number, and optional message. Cancellation deadlines apply; preserve cancelled/no-show records. Operators filter bookings by date/staff and can cancel or mark no-show.

Store a required nonnegative booking-time price in the shop's currency (whole JPY or USD with two decimal places); later service price changes do not alter history. Optional messages are up to 2,000 characters and appear in operator bookings, customer timeline, personal history, and notification emails.

Saved-booking refund policies show explicit date/time ranges computed from the appointment start and snapshotted tiers. Each deadline is inclusive; the next range starts strictly after it, with the final range including times after the appointment starts. Show years and shop time-zone offsets/abbreviations to distinguish DST transitions and repeated local hours. A single zero-hour tier means any time. This display does not change the saved percentages, captured amount, or refund calculation.

## Cancellations and discounted appointments

Customers use one **Cancel** action. Eligible paid bookings automatically accept a replacement reservation, with an expected refund, a larger refund if someone books, and a booking deadline shown before confirmation. The server rechecks both amounts and the deadline; changed terms require confirmation, and retries never create duplicate requests. Full-refund periods, custom refund rates above 70%, excluded/deleted menus and appointments too close to their start use normal immediate cancellation. Unpaid bookings also cancel normally.

Show “Cancellation received” and “If someone else books this appointment, your refund increases to …”, without transfer terminology. Refunds are finalized when someone books or the deadline passes; payment started before the deadline may still need confirmation. **Undo cancellation and restore booking** is available only before the deadline and before someone else starts paying. A shop cancellation still refunds the current holder in full, except while a replacement payment is in progress.

Prices come deterministically from the original snapshotted policy and the current customer's payment: equal-rate tiers form one zone, choose the largest of the fixed 50%, 30%, 10% discounts that improves recovery over cancellation by at least 20 percentage points. If none qualifies, use normal cancellation. With the default 24h/100%, 12h/70%, 1h/40%, then 0% refund policy, full-refund cancellations are immediate; the other zones sell at 10% off until 12 hours before, 30% off until one hour before, and half price until 10 minutes before. Unsold settlements refund 70%, 40% or 0% respectively. Custom policies use the same rate-based selection and never cross zone boundaries. Round down in minor units. If nobody books, automatically cancel and settle the expected refund shown when cancellation was received; do not reduce it as time passes. A checkout started before the deadline has its own 10-minute hold. Expiry and settlement end the request; do not relist or roll into the next zone (confirmed in the 2026-11-05 follow-up).

New customers see **Discounted appointments** and **Book this appointment**, with fixed service, staff, time, price and refund policy. Capture a new payment, atomically persist the new customer and refund intent, then refund the same amount to the previous customer; the shop's total remains unchanged. Further cancellations use the new customer's own payment. Never expose previous customer identities, messages, history or internal request keys to customers. Keep original messages only in operator history. A later shop cancellation refunds only the current customer's payment, with no additional refund to the original customer.

Notify the cancelling customer on receipt, replacement booking and deadline settlement; notify the new customer of confirmation and the shop of the replacement. Operators see pending cancellations, holder history and refunds, and sales are recorded in history.md. Event notifications, a history intent and acknowledgements commit together and retry every minute after failure, even after a sale or another holder change. Shop-history updates and their acknowledgements commit separately under the configuration lock; booking/payment operations never wait for Drive or overwrite concurrently published shop data. Durable payment journals recover lost captures/refunds without duplicate charges or refunds.

Storybook includes 12 customer and 3 operator states with interactive cancellation, undo, discounted bookings and refund retry. Payments are simulated without external navigation. Offline previews retain normal cancellation without replacement offers. Waitlist notification, cross-shop listings, negotiated prices and partial transfers remain out of scope.

## Opening alerts

Storybook's Customer screens → Opening alerts includes an expanded registration form and saved alerts with stop actions. The manual explains registration, viewing and stopping alerts with Japanese and English screenshots, and states that alerts do not reserve appointments: bookings are first come, first served.

Signed-in registered customers can save a date and time range (for example 5/17 10:00–20:00) from **Tell me when a time opens up** under the time list. The service, add-ons and staff come from the booking form. Dates must be in the booking window, each customer may keep 10 alerts, and identical alerts are not duplicated. Shops with PayPal also offer **Only discounted appointments**.

Ordinary openings use the same availability check as booking, including payment holds, Calendar and hours, so changed shifts count as well as cancellations. A discounted appointment matches on the base service (add-ons are ignored), any requested staff member and the whole appointment fitting the range. Listings someone is paying for and the customer's own cancelled booking are excluded.

Checks run every minute, reading Calendar outside the mail lock. What was open at registration is not mailed; only openings that appear later are. Alerts go to the customer alone, are first come, first served, and hold nothing. A time that fills and reopens is mailed again. Ordinary openings are limited to one mail an hour, because payment holds make them flap; a new discounted appointment is mailed without waiting. A discounted appointment already mailed is not mailed again when someone else's payment lapses. While a Calendar is unreadable nothing is decided, and the next check retries. Notices never reveal the previous customer, use the language chosen at registration, and link to booking and My page.

My page lists alerts with **Stop alert**. An alert is removed when the customer books inside its range after registering (a booking they already held, or one awaiting a replacement, does not count) or when the range has passed. If the shop stops offering the service or add-on, the alert is removed and the customer is told by email. Data exports include alerts. Offline previews can practice saving and stopping one but send no mail.

## PayPal checks, alerts and proposals

- The AI never writes to PayPal. It can only read; refunds and retries run through the existing server code (`refundQuote()`, fixed `PayPal-Request-Id`, the per-minute reconciliation) and always need the owner's approval on screen. The AI never calculates amounts; every amount shown comes from the server or PayPal. Only the shop's own transactions are visible.
- For payment, refund or transaction questions in Consult & ask, the conversation layer sets `paypal: true` on `investigate`. Research then gets read-only tools: find a booking's order, capture and refund IDs in the ledger; read an order, capture or refund from PayPal by ID; and search transactions for up to 31 days. Tools are rebuilt from the shop's ledger for each consultation, and IDs not in the ledger are refused before PayPal is called. Results carry status, amount, IDs and times only, never customer email addresses, with amounts in normal units (USD 49.50 → 49.5).
- When the ledger and PayPal disagree, the answer states the difference and cites the PayPal record (for example `paypal:refund:ID`). If a record cannot be read, it says so instead of guessing.
- Seed PayPal research with the shop's recent ledger before the first model call. An unfiltered lookup returns recent records; date ranges use payment/refund activity times, never appointment dates. Verify a recent refund by its ledger refund ID and return its ID, PayPal status, amount, currency and source. Individual lookups can succeed without Transaction Search permissions; do not repeat an unauthorized search with different dates.
- Check now shows completion and the check time even with zero findings. Summarize counts in the current display language when a saved summary uses another language. Do not create artificial pending items in a healthy judge shop.
- The background loop checks every 60 minutes (`PAYPAL_ALERT_INTERVAL_MINUTES`) and reports: needs review (`manual_review`, urgent), payment not confirmed (`capturing` for 15 minutes or more, or an unknown capture result, urgent), ledger and PayPal disagree (urgent), refund stalled (`retrying`/`pending` for 2 hours or more, attention), awaiting funds (`awaiting_funds`, attention) and unsold discounted slot (2 hours or less before its deadline, check). `PAYPAL_REFUND_STALL_HOURS` and `PAYPAL_CAPTURE_STALL_MINUTES` change the thresholds. PayPal is queried only for open refunds and payments active in the last 7 days (at most 30); `REFUNDED`/`PARTIALLY_REFUNDED` captures are expected. `awaiting_approval` is excluded because a checkout expires in 10 minutes. Checks from Consult & ask use the ledger rules only and keep the previous PayPal results.
- Detection uses fixed rules; the AI only writes a 2–4 sentence summary. **Needs attention** in Consult & ask shows the count and list, and each item can start a consultation with its booking and PayPal IDs. **Check now** runs a check at any time.
- Urgent items are emailed when they appear or change; others go out once a day as a digest at the hour set in **Notification settings** (9:00 in the shop's time zone by default). Email can be turned off there, and an item is not sent again until its state changes. Judge shops send no email and show notices on screen only.
- Fixable items carry a proposal. The only operations are: retry a refund (`retrying`/`pending`), re-sync the state from PayPal (on a disagreement), complete a manual review (when PayPal shows the refund finished; without a refund ID, the owner is directed to the PayPal dashboard), and close an unsold discounted slot at the refund quoted at cancellation. The server decides operations and amounts; the AI cannot propose a new refund or change an amount. An answer shows proposals for the bookings and PayPal IDs it mentions, otherwise up to five, most urgent first.
- Only the owner can execute a proposal. The server takes the booking lock, reads that booking from PayPal again and refuses with the current state if anything changed since the proposal. Retries reuse the original request ID, so nothing is refunded twice. Results are recorded in `history.md` and the operation log and returned to Consult & ask.
- Out of scope: AI-created refunds or amount changes, PayPal Disputes and webhook signature verification, and notifications other than email.

### Inquiry conversations and manual reception

An inquiry becomes part of the existing email-based CRM when it has an email address.
- Store append-only conversation records separately as `inquiryInteraction` business records with shop, inquiry, channel, text, actor and time. Phone, customer-message and internal-note entries never enqueue mail. Every new email reply must include `previousEmail`; missing or changed recipients return 409 without saving or sending. Undelivered replies go to the corrected contact; they become failed when the contact email is removed or after about a day of failed sends. Shops without Google reject replies.
